Welcome to Ultimate Frisbee

Ultimate is a non-contact team sport played with a flying disc. The object of the game is to score points by passing the disc to a player in the opposing end zone. Rooted in a strong spirit of fair play, the sport has traditionally been self-refereed even at the highest levels of competition.

Ultimate was developed in 1968 by students at Columbia High School in Maplewood, New Jersey. It is now played by millions worldwide, with national and international competitions held regularly at the amateur, semi-professional, and professional levels.

How to Play

Two teams of seven players compete on a rectangular field with end zones at each end. A team scores when one of its players catches a pass inside the opposing end zone. The disc may only move by passing — the player holding it cannot run. Any incomplete, intercepted, or out-of-bounds pass causes an immediate turnover.

Basic Rules

  • Seven players per side on the field
  • No running with the disc
  • Turnovers on incomplete passes, interceptions, or out-of-bounds throws
  • No physical contact permitted
  • Score by catching in the opposing end zone
