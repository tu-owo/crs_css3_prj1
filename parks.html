Featured Parks & Venues

Ultimate Frisbee is played in parks and open spaces all over the world. Below are some of the most celebrated venues in the sport, from championship tournament fields to local community parks where the game was first discovered.

Maplewood Memorial Park

Birthplace of the sport. Columbia High School students played the very first organized game of Ultimate here in 1968. The wide open grass fields made it the perfect proving ground for the flying disc.

University of Michigan Diag

Ann Arbor's iconic central plaza has seen thousands of informal Ultimate games over the decades. Michigan Ultimate teams have long used the surrounding fields for practice, producing top collegiate talent.

Magnuson Park, Seattle

Home to multiple Seattle-area club teams including Sockeye, Magnuson Park features wide, well-maintained grass fields that host regional qualifiers and tournaments every summer.

Golden Gate Park, San Francisco

The Polo Fields within Golden Gate Park have been the regular practice home of Revolver, arguably the most decorated club team in North American Ultimate history.

Why These Fields Matter

Great Ultimate venues share common traits: open, flat grass surfaces of at least 70 by 40 meters, accessible by public transit, and enough space for multiple fields side by side during tournaments. The parks below offer those amenities along with a rich history in the sport.

Open Grass

Flat, well-maintained turf ideal for barefoot play and disc flight.

Spectator Areas

Sideline space for fans, coaches, and substitutes during games.

Facilities

Restrooms, water access, and parking for tournament-sized events.

Evening Play

Some venues offer lighting for late-night summer pickup games.
